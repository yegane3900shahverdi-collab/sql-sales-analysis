# Key Findings – SQL Sales Analysis

## 1. Revenue Performance
Total revenue across the analyzed period was **1,265,793.04**.

## 2. Annual Revenue Trends
- 2014: 208,083.97
- 2015: 617,085.20
- 2016: 440,623.87

Revenue increased by **196.57%** from 2014 to 2015, followed by a **28.60% decrease** in 2016.

## 3. Customer Analysis
- 89 unique customers were identified.
- Customer order frequency was analyzed.
- Top 10 customers were ranked by revenue.

## 4. Product Analysis
- Products were ranked based on total revenue.
- Top 10 revenue-generating products were identified.

## 5. Business Insight
The business experienced strong revenue growth in 2015, followed by a decline in 2016. Further analysis of customer purchasing patterns and product performance could help investigate the reasons behind this change.
